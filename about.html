About CEC Contractors

We are a family-rooted contracting company: formerly Cutting Edge Carpentry, now CEC Contractors — building on 25 years of family craftsmanship with a fresh name and the same standards.

Our story

CEC Contractors continues a legacy of honest work and meticulous job sites. For 25 years combined our team has refined trim, restoration, and commercial interiors; we have operated as CEC Contractors for four years, pairing modern scheduling with old-school pride in the fit and finish.

CEC Carpentry may appear on older estimates or references — it reflects the same crew and values you have trusted for years.
